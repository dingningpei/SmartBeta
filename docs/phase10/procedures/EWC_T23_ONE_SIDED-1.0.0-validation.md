## 1. Primary source and equations

The procedure is the one-sided, **equal-weighted-cosine (EWC) long-run-variance
t-test** of Lazarus, Lewis, Stock & Watson (2018), used here with the frozen
fixed-`nu` rule `nu = round(T^(2/3))` as the number of cosine terms and a
Student-t reference with `nu` degrees of freedom. It is the candidate frozen by
PA-0 v2 (`docs/phase10/procedures/pa0_v2_ewc_t23_candidate_spec.md`); the
superseded PA-0 v1 candidate (`NONOVERLAP_T_ONE_SIDED`, a plain one-sample
t-test) stays NOT CERTIFIED and is not admitted.

Let `x = (x_1, ..., x_T)` be the per-formation net long-short return series,
`s in {+1, -1}` the precommitted direction, and `theta' = s * theta` the
direction-adjusted estimand. With `z_t = s * x_t`, `zbar = (1/T) * sum_t z_t`,
and

    nu = round(T^(2/3)),

the statistic is

- `Lambda_j = sqrt(2/T) * sum_{t=1..T} cos(pi * j * (t - 1/2) / T) *
  (z_t - zbar)`, for `j = 1, ..., nu`;
- `omega_hat^2 = (1/nu) * sum_{j=1..nu} Lambda_j^2`;
- `t = sqrt(T) * zbar / omega_hat`;
- `p = 1 - F_{t,nu}(t)` (one-sided, `ASYMPTOTIC` validity);
- `UB = zbar + t_{1-bound_alpha,nu} * omega_hat / sqrt(T)`
  (one-sided upper confidence bound at level `1 - bound_alpha`).

The null is `H0: theta' <= 0`. `nu` is fixed by the rule and is **not** a
procedure parameter: `nu = 8` at `T = 24`, `11` at `T = 36`, `15` at `T = 60`.
The procedure is closed form and deterministic — no bandwidth selection, no
resampling, no fallback and no post-data repair.

### Data-generating process (the estimand's construction)

The data-generating process is identical to the PA-0 v1 study, so the two
candidates are compared on the same estimand construction. The per-formation
series is the net long-short return of two equal quintile legs of `N` names.
Each name obeys a one-factor model with a unit-variance common factor `f` and
idiosyncratic noise `eps ~ N(0, 1)`; the long-short legs carry a unit net
exposure to `f`, and the idiosyncratic noise averages over the `N/5` names of
each leg. The net long-short return is therefore

    LS_t = (delta + f_t + u_t) / sqrt(1 + v),   u_t ~ N(0, v),  v = 10 / N,

where `f` is a stationary unit-variance AR(1) with coefficient `rho_1` and the
cell's innovation distribution, and `v = 10/N` is the exact variance of
(mean of one quintile leg - mean of the other) of unit-variance idiosyncratic
noise. The `1/sqrt(1+v)` normalization sets the series variance to one, so
`delta = sharpe / sqrt(12)` is the mean that realises an annual Sharpe of
`sharpe` at unit monthly volatility. The reduction of the quintile long-short
to `f + u` is exact in distribution (leg means of i.i.d. Gaussian noise are
Gaussian) and is checked against a literal `N`-name simulation in the test
suite (`test_mc_reduction_matches_literal_name_simulation`).
